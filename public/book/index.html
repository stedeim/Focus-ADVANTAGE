“My problem was never effort. It was architecture.”

In August 2012, Deimira Baidoo went back to Ghana, where he was born, and set up a gold business. It went terribly badly. He came back to Canada with about four hundred dollars to his name. He was down, depressed, and working at a bank, where he was working very hard just to stay afloat and achieving very little of what mattered.

He had plenty of drive, but it was scattered. He was trying to do too many things at once. It looked like ambition. It kept the important things from getting done.

Trying harder would not fix it. He needed a different structure.

This book is that structure: how a mind is designed, how an environment is designed, and what happens when the two meet. Twelve chapters, from seeing the cost to living the focused life.
